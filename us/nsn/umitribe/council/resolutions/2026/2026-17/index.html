2026-17. Codification of Unassigned Adopted Laws

A Resolution to designate the Title and Chapter assignments of the Upper Mattaponi Tribal Code of Laws for unassigned adopted laws since 2024.

WHEREAS, the Upper Mattaponi Tribal Council ("Council") is vested with the legislative power of the Upper Mattaponi Tribe ("Tribe") and has the authority to enact laws pursuant to Article VII, Section 4(a) of the Constitution of the Upper Mattaponi Tribe ("Constitution"); and

WHEREAS, in 2024, the Tribe adopted Resolution 2024-02, titled "Authority to Enter Into Exclusivity Agreements for Economic Development," but failed to designate where the Resolution should be codified in the Upper Mattaponi Tribal Code of Law ("UMTC"); and

WHEREAS, in 2024, the Tribe adopted Resolution 2024-11, titled "Emergency Assistance Fund Authorization Act of 2024," but failed to designate where the Resolution should be codified in the UMTC; and

WHEREAS, in 2025, the Tribe adopted Resolution 2025-15, titled "The Due Diligence Act of 2025," but failed to designate where the Resolution should be codified in the UMTC; and

WHEREAS, in 2025, the Tribe adopted Bill 2025-02, titled "The Upper Mattaponi Ethics Law," but failed to designate where the Bill should be codified in the UMTC; and

WHEREAS, in 2026, the Tribe adopted Resolution 2026-13, titled "Commercial Leasing Law for Tribal Uses," and indicated that it should be codified in Title 42 of the UMTC, but its current number conflicts with previous adopted laws codified in Title 42 of the UMTC; and

WHEREAS, the Council finds that specific locations for codification should be provided for the foregoing laws.

NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED that the Upper Mattaponi Tribal Council hereby directs that the following laws be codified in the UMTC as follows:

(1) Resolution 2024-02, titled "Authority to Enter Into Exclusivity Agreements for Economic Development," shall be codified as Chapter 3 of Title 1 (The Chief and Assistant Chief) of the UMTC, and renumbered accordingly as Sections 301 through 304.

(2) Resolution 2024-11, titled "Emergency Assistance Fund Authorization Act of 2024," shall be codified as Chapter 1 of Title 5 (General Provisions) of the UMTC, and renumbered accordingly as Sections 101 through 105.

(3) Resolution 2025-15, titled "The Due Diligence Act of 2025," shall be codified as Chapter 13 of Title 4 (Administration) of the UMTC, and renumbered accordingly as Sections 1301 through 1305.

(4) Bill 2025-02, titled "The Upper Mattaponi Ethics Law," shall be codified as Chapter 14 of Title 4 (Administration) of the UMTC, and renumbered accordingly as Sections 1401 through 1412.

(5) Resolution 2026-13, titled "Commercial Leasing Law for Tribal Uses," shall be codified as Chapter 5 of Title 42 (Tribal Land, Buildings, Property, and Works) of the UMTC, and renumbered accordingly as Sections 501 through 505.

CERTIFICATION

The foregoing Resolution was duly voted upon by the Upper Mattaponi Tribal Council on the 21st day of May, 2026, at a General Meeting with a vote of 7 in favor and 0 opposed, 0 abstaining, and 0 absent, pursuant to the authority vested in the Council by the Constitution of the Upper Mattaponi Tribe.
