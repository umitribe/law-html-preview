2026-02. Authorization of USET Contract with Limited Waiver of Sovereign Immunity

A Resolution to authorize execution of a contract with United South and Eastern Tribes, Inc. (USET) and to provide a limited waiver of sovereign immunity for purposes thereof.

At a duly called General Meeting of the Upper Mattaponi Tribal Council held this 24th day of January 2026, the following law was adopted by a vote of the Council as reflected herein:

WHEREAS, the Upper Mattaponi Tribe (the "Tribe") is a federally recognized Indian Tribe with inherent sovereign authority, including the authority to enter into contracts and agreements; and

WHEREAS, pursuant to Article XIII, Section 1 the Constitution of the Upper Mattaponi Tribe (the "Constitution"), only the Upper Mattaponi Tribal Council (the "Council") and Chief may authorize a waiver of the Tribe's sovereign immunity, and any such waiver must be specific, limited in scope and duration, and in writing; and

WHEREAS, the Tribe has the opportunity to enter into a Contract for Products and/or Services (the "Contract") with United South and Eastern Tribes, Inc. ("USET") for the installation of a rainwater catchment and irrigation system and related community education activities; and

WHEREAS, the purpose of the Contract is to address water and soil conservation issues, reduce erosion and runoff, and support the Tribe's food sovereignty and agricultural sustainability goals; and

WHEREAS, the Contract includes provisions requiring a limited waiver of the Tribe's sovereign immunity solely for purposes of dispute resolution arising under the Contract; and

WHEREAS, the Council has reviewed the terms of the Contract and finds that entering into the Contract and granting a limited waiver of sovereign immunity as set forth herein is in the best interests of the Tribe;

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED that the Upper Mattaponi Tribal Council hereby authorizes the Tribe to enter into the Contract with United South and Eastern Tribes, Inc., substantially in the form presented to the Council; and

BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED that the Chief of the Upper Mattaponi Tribe, or his designee, is hereby authorized to execute the Contract and any related documents necessary to carry out its purposes; and

BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED that, solely for the purpose of resolving disputes arising under or related to the Contract, the Tribe hereby grants a limited waiver of its sovereign immunity, subject to the following express limitations:

1. The waiver is strictly limited to claims arising directly under the Contract;

2. The waiver does not extend to any officer, employee, or agent of the Tribe; and

3. All sovereign immunity not expressly waived is reserved.

CERTIFICATION

The foregoing Resolution was duly voted upon by the Council on the 24th day of January, 2025, at a General Meeting with a vote of 7 in favor and 0 opposed, 0 abstaining, and 0 absent, pursuant to the authority vested in the Council by the Constitution of the Upper Mattaponi Tribe.
