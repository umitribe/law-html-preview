2026-01. Grant Authorization Amendment Act of 2026

An Act related to the authority of the Chief or their designee to apply for and receive grant funding; amending Title 1, Section 203 of the Upper Mattaponi Tribal Code of Law, which relates to the Chief's or their designee's authority to negotiate and enter into Self-Determination 638-Contracts; expanding authority for the Chief or their designee to clarify that the Executive Branch may apply for, and receive, grant funding on behalf of the Tribe; and providing an effective date.

WHEREAS, the Upper Mattaponi Tribal Council ("Council") is vested with the legislative power of the Upper Mattaponi Tribe ("Tribe") and has the authority to enact laws pursuant to Article VII, Section 4(a) of the Constitution of the Upper Mattaponi Tribe ("Constitution"); and

WHEREAS, in 2023 the Council adopted the Self-Determination 638-Contracts Act of 2023, which authorized the Chief of the Tribe to negotiate and execute Self-Determination 638-Contracts on behalf of the Tribe. The Act has been codified as Chapter 2 of Title 1 of the Upper Mattaponi Tribal Code of Law ("UMTC"); and

WHEREAS, Title 1, Section 203 of the UMTC authorizes the Chief of the Tribe to negotiate and execute Self-Determination 638-Contracts with the federal government and the BIA on behalf of the Tribe; and

WHEREAS, in order to provide clarity regarding the Executive Branch's authority to apply for and enter into grant funding opportunities, the Council believes Title 1, Section 203 of the UMTC should be amended.

BE IT ENACTED BY THE PEOPLE OF THE UPPER MATTAPONI TRIBE:

Amend Title 1, 203

Section 1. AMENDATORY Title 1, Section 203 of the Upper Mattaponi Tribal Code of Law is amended to read as follows:

203 Authorization to Enter Into 638-Contracts

The Council hereby authorizes and directs the Chief or their designee to negotiate and execute 638-Contracts, and apply for and receive grant funds, with the federal government and the BIA on behalf of the Tribe. The Chairman Chief or their designee shall be authorized to execute all necessary documents or contracts necessary to effectuate the intent of this chapter.

Section 2. This act shall be effective immediately upon its passage and approval according to the process defined in the Constitution.

CERTIFICATION

The foregoing Act was duly voted upon by the Upper Mattaponi Tribal Council on the 21st day of May, 2026, at a General Meeting with a vote of 7 in favor and 0 opposed, 0 abstaining, and 0 absent, pursuant to the authority vested in the Council by the Constitution of the Upper Mattaponi Tribe.
