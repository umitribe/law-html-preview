2026-02. Upper Mattaponi Cannabis Regulatory Amendment Act of 2026

An Act related to the regulation of Cannabis; amending Title 28 of the Upper Mattaponi Tribal Code of Laws; renaming the Cannabis Control Commission to the Cannabis Control Authority; clarifying the scope of sovereign immunity of Commissioners; amending financial provisions; various minor amendments; and providing an effective date.

At a duly called General Meeting of the Upper Mattaponi Tribe Council held this 27th day of June, 2026, the following Law was adopted.

WHEREAS, the Upper Mattaponi Tribal Council ("Council") is vested with the legislative power of the Upper Mattaponi Tribe ("Tribe") and has the authority to enact laws pursuant to Article VII, Section 4(a) of the Constitution of the Upper Mattaponi Tribe ("Constitution"); and

WHEREAS, in March 2026 the Council adopted the Upper Mattaponi Cannabis Regulatory Act (Resolution 2026-11), which regulated the growth and sale of cannabis within the Upper Mattaponi Tribe. The Act has been codified in Title 28 of the Upper Mattaponi Tribal Code of Laws ("UMTC"); and

WHEREAS, following adoption of the Upper Mattaponi Cannabis Regulatory Act the Tribe requested consideration of amendments regarding the name of regulatory agency, clarifying the scope of sovereign immunity for Commissioners, amending financial provisions, and other minor amendments.

BE IT ENACTED BY THE COUNCIL OF THE UPPER MATTAPONI TRIBE:

Amend Title 28, 100

Section 1. AMENDATORY Title 28, Section 100 of the Upper Mattaponi Tribal Code of Laws is amended to read as follows:

100 Short Title

This enactmentAct shall be known as "The Upper Mattaponi Cannabis Regulatory Act."

Amend Title 28, 102

Section 2. AMENDATORY Title 28, Section 102(2) of the Upper Mattaponi Tribal Code of Laws is amended to read as follows:

102 Purpose

(2) Provide that citizens of the Upper Mattaponi Tribe, members of other federally recognized tribes, Virginia residents, and other persons have the ability to lawfully obtain, purchase, receive, possess, and use of all Cannabis products in accordance with this Act and any regulations adopted by the Upper Mattaponi Cannabis Control AuthorityCommission;

Amend Title 28, 103

Section 3. AMENDATORY Title 28, Section 103 of the Upper Mattaponi Tribal Code of Laws is amended to read as follows:

103 Authority and Sovereign Immunity

(A) This titleAct is enacted pursuant to the inherent sovereign authority of the Upper Mattaponi Tribe and pursuant to the Constitution of the Upper Mattaponi Tribe, which confers all Legislative power to the Upper Mattaponi Tribe in the Council of the Upper Mattaponi Tribe, and expressly empowers the Council "to create departments, boards, and commissions located in the Executive branch of government by law" (Article VII § 4(d)).

(B) Nothing in this Act shall be construed to limit or waive any aspect of the Upper Mattaponi Tribe's sovereign authority to regulate activities conducted in whole or in part within the boundaries of the Upper Mattaponi Tribe.

(C) The Upper Mattaponi Cannabis Control AuthorityCommission established by this Act as well as any Commissioner, duly nominated by the Chief and confirmed by a majority vote of the Council, shall be deemed to be an arm of the Upper Mattaponi Tribe and is protected by the same immunity from suit as Tribe pursuant its own sovereign immunity.

Amend Title 28, 106

Section 4. AMENDATORY Title 28, Section 106 of the Upper Mattaponi Tribal Code of Laws is amended, in part, to read as follows:

106 Definitions

For the purposes of this titleAct, the following terms shall have the following meanings:

Section 5. AMENDATORY Title 28, Section 106(P) & Title 28, Section 106(Z) of the Upper Mattaponi Tribal Code of Laws is amended, in part, to read as follows:

106 Definitions

(P) "Commission" means the Upper Mattaponi Cannabis Control AuthorityCommission.

(Z) "Executive Director" means the Executive Director of the Upper Mattaponi Tribal Cannabis Control Commission appointed pursuant to this Act.

Amend Title 28, Chapter 2

Section 6. AMENDATORY Title 28, Chapter 2 of the Upper Mattaponi Tribal Code of Laws is amended to be "Upper Mattaponi Cannabis Control Authority."

Amend Title 28, 200

Section 7. AMENDATORY Title 28, Section 200 of the Upper Mattaponi Tribal Code of Laws is amended to read as follows:

200 Establishment

(A) The Upper Mattaponi Tribe hereby establishes the Upper Mattaponi Cannabis Control CommissionAuthority, hereinafter referred to as the "Commission.".

(B) The Council of the Upper Mattaponi Tribe hereby assigns oversight and regulatory powers to the Upper Mattaponi Cannabis Control CommissionAuthority to implement the provisions of this Act.

(C) The Upper Mattaponi Cannabis Control CommissionAuthority shall be independent of, and act independently and autonomously from, the Council of the Upper Mattaponi Tribe in all matters within its purview. No prior or subsequent review by the Council of the Upper Mattaponi Tribe of any action of the Commission is required or permitted except as otherwise provided in this Act or the Constitution of the Upper Mattaponi Tribe.

(D) The Upper Mattaponi Cannabis Control CommissionAuthority shall be governed by the provisions of this Act and any other relevant laws of the Upper Mattaponi Tribe.

Amend Title 28, 205

Section 8. AMENDATORY Title 28, Section 205(A) of the Upper Mattaponi Tribal Code of Laws is amended to read as follows:

205 Executive Director

(A) The position of Executive Director of the Upper Mattaponi Cannabis Control CommissionAuthority is hereby created.

Amend Title 28, 206

Section 9. AMENDATORY Title 28, Section 206 of the Upper Mattaponi Tribal Code of Laws is amended to read as follows:

206 Organization, Expenses, Employees

(A) The Executive Director may, subject to the approval of the Commission:

(1) Establish, and from time to time alter, such a plan of organization as they may deem expedient;

(2) Acquire such furnishings, equipment, supplies, stationery, books, motor vehicles and other things as they may deem necessary in carrying out the functions of the Commission; and

(3) Incur such other expenses, within the budget available to the Commission, as they may deem necessary.

(B) Except as otherwise provided in this Act, all costs of administration incurred by the Commission must be paid out of the Tribe's operating budget in the same manner as other governmental expenses are paid.

(C) The Executive Director shall organize the work of the Commission in such a way to secure maximum efficiency in the conduct of the Commission and make recommendations to the Commission for the establishment of Commission departments, staff organization or committees to ensure efficient and defined roles and responsibilities.

(D) The Executive Director may employ such clerical or expert assistance as may be required.

(E) Persons employed by the Commission may be assigned to stations, offices, or locations selected by the Executive Director both within and outside the jurisdiction of the Tribe where, in the judgment of the Executive Director, it is necessary to maintain personnel to protect, investigate, and ensure the safe and lawful conduct of the Cannabis industry on Tribal lands.

(F) Any person assigned to a station, office or location as provided in paragraph (E) of this section shall be eligible to receive a per diem allowance when the business of the Commission takes the person away from the particular station, office, or location to which they are assigned in accordance with the Tribe's travel policies.

Amend Title 28, 410

Section 10. AMENDATORY Title 28, Section 410(D) of the Upper Mattaponi Tribal Code of Laws is amended to read as follows:

410 Fees

(D) Any revenue generated from the fees imposed pursuant to this section:

(1) Must be expended first to pay the costs of the Commission in carrying out the provisions of this Act; and

(2) If any excess revenue remains after paying the costs described in paragraph (D)(1) of this section, such excess revenue must be paid as instructed by the Department of Finance so as not to comingle such revenue with the Tribe's other monies.

Amend Title 28, 601

Section 11. AMENDATORY Title 28, Section 601(B) of the Upper Mattaponi Tribal Code of Laws is amended to read as follows:

601 Enforcement of Contracts

(B) The Courts of the Upper Mattaponi Tribe shall have jurisdiction over any party to a contract related to the operation of Cannabis facilities under this Act for the purposes of enforcement of such contract unless otherwise agreed to in writing by the parties and properly approved pursuant to the Constitution of the Upper Mattaponi Tribe.

Section 12. This act shall be effective immediately upon its passage and approval according to process defined in the Constitution.

CERTIFICATION

The foregoing Act was duly voted upon by the Council on the 27th day of June, 2026, at a General Meeting with a vote of 6 in favor and 0 opposed, 0 abstaining, and 1 absent, pursuant to the authority vested in the Council by the Constitution of the Upper Mattaponi Tribe.
