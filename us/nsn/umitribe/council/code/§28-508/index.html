Upper Mattaponi Tribal Code of Law

508 Random Laboratory Assurance Checks

(A) The Commission may establish a program to ensure the integrity of all testing performed by a Cannabis laboratory by subjecting each such laboratory to random laboratory assurance checks.

(B) If the Commission establishes a program pursuant to paragraph (A) of this Section, the Commission shall as a part of the program:

(1) Collect samples of Cannabis or Cannabis products from Cannabis facilities that have already been tested by Cannabis laboratories in amounts deemed sufficient by the Commission;

(2) Remove identifying characteristics from and randomize such samples; and

(3) Provide each Cannabis laboratory with a sample for analysis.

(C) A Cannabis laboratory that receives a sample from the Commission shall perform such quality assurance tests upon the sample as the Commission may require. Such tests may include, without limitation:

(1) Screening the sample for pesticides, heavy metals, chemical residues, herbicides, growth regulators and microbial analysis;

(2) A potency analysis to test for and quantify the presence of the following cannabinoids:

(a) THC;

(b) Tetrahydrocannabinolic acid;

(c) Cannabidiol;

(d) Cannabidiolic acid;

(e) Cannabinol; and

(f) Such other quality assurance tests that the Commission may require.

(3) If the Commission establishes a program pursuant to paragraph (A) of this section, the Commission shall adopt regulations necessary to carry out the program. Such regulations include:

(a) Must require each Cannabis laboratory to perform a random laboratory assurance check at least once every six (6) months but not more frequently than once every three (3) months; and

(b) May modify the procedures and requirements set forth in this section if the Commission determines that advances in science necessitate such a modification;

(4) As used in this section, "random laboratory assurance check" means the evaluation of the performance of a Cannabis laboratory in conducting quality assurance tests upon a sample if required by the Commission under the program established pursuant to paragraph (A) of this section; and

(5) Consumption within a Cannabis facility. The Commission may adopt regulations prescribing the requirements and standards for the consumption of Cannabis and Cannabis products within a Cannabis facility.

(Res. No. 2026-11.)
