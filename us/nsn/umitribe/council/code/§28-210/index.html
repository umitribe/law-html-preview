Upper Mattaponi Tribal Code of Law

210 Audits of Licenses, Standards, Annual Report

(A) The Commission shall cause to be conducted independent financial and operational audits of the accounts, funds, programs, activities, and functions of every facility licensee.

(B) A licensee shall make available to the Commission all books, accounts, claims, reports, vouchers, and other records requested by the Commission in connection with an audit conducted pursuant to paragraph (A) of this section.

(C) If a licensee refuses to produce any of the records described in paragraph (B) of this section, the Commission may petition the Upper Mattaponi Trial Court to order the licensee to produce the requested records. The Trial Court shall order the production of all such records upon a finding that the requested records are within the scope of the audit.

(D) Annual audits shall conform to generally accepted auditing principles, the audit firm must be an independent certified public accountant and must be licensed by a state board of accountancy in accordance with 25 CFR § 571.12(b).

(E) If any audit report of the accounts, funds, programs, activities, and functions of a facility licensee contains adverse or critical audit results, the Commission shall require the facility licensee to respond, in writing, to the results of the audit. A facility licensee shall provide such response to the Commission not more than fifteen (15) days after receiving a request from the Commission.

(F) Each year, the Commission shall submit to the Chief and Speaker of the Council a report concerning the audits conducted pursuant to this section for the preceding year. The report must include, without limitation:

(1) The number of audits performed pursuant to this section in the preceding year;

(2) A summary of the findings of the audits, provided that complete audits are provided to the Council within fifteen (15) days upon request of the Speaker; and

(3) The cost of each audit.

(Res. No. 2026-11.)
