Upper Mattaponi Tribal Code of Law

506 Requirements Concerning Cannabis Products

(A) Each Cannabis facility shall, in consultation with the Commission, cooperate to ensure that all Cannabis products offered for sale:

(1) Are labeled clearly and unambiguously:

(a) As Cannabis with the words "THIS IS A CANNABIS PRODUCT" in bold type; and

(b) As required by the other provisions of this Act.

(2) Are not presented in packaging that contains an image of a cartoon character, mascot, action figure, balloon, or toy, except that such an item may appear in the logo of the Cannabis facility which produced the product;

(3) Are regulated and sold on the basis of the concentration of THC in the products and not by weight;

(4) Are packaged in a Child-resistant manner.

(5) Are packaged and labeled in such a manner as to allow tracking by way of an inventory control system;

(6) Are not packaged and labeled in a manner which is modeled after a brand of products primarily consumed by or marketed to children;

(7) Are labeled in a manner which indicates the amount of THC in the product, measured in milligrams, and includes a statement that the product contains Cannabis and its potency was tested with an allowable variance of the amount determined by the Commission by regulation; and

(8) Are not labeled or marketed as candy.

(B) A Cannabis manufacturing facility shall not produce Cannabis products in any form that:

(1) Is or appears to be a lollipop;

(2) Bears the likeness or contains characteristics of a real or fictional person, animal or fruit, including, without limitation, a caricature, cartoon or artistic rendering;

(3) Is modeled after a brand of products primarily consumed by or marketed to children; and

(4) Is made by applying concentrated Cannabis to a commercially available candy or snack food item other than dried fruit, nuts, or granola.

(C) A Cannabis manufacturing facility shall:

(1) Seal any Cannabis product that consists of cookies or brownies in a bag or other container which is not transparent;

(2) Affix a label to each Cannabis product which includes without limitation, in a manner which must not mislead consumers, the following information:

(a) The words "Keep out of reach of children;"

(b) A list of all ingredients used in the Cannabis product;

(c) A list of all allergens in the Cannabis product; and

(d) The total content of THC measured in milligrams.

(3) Maintain a hand washing area with hot water, soap and disposable towels which is located away from any area in which Cannabis products are cooked or otherwise prepared;

(4) Require each person who handles Cannabis or Cannabis products to restrain his or her hair, wear clean clothing, and keep his or her fingernails neatly trimmed;

(5) Package all Cannabis products produced by the Cannabis manufacturing facility on the premises of the Cannabis manufacturing facility;

(6) To strictly adhere to applicable tribal, state, or federal food service sanitation laws and regulations;

(7) Require each person to hold and maintain a food handlers permit issued by the Tribe or similar permit issued by the state or federal government;

(D) A Cannabis facility shall not engage in advertising that in any way makes Cannabis or Cannabis products appeal to children, including without limitation, advertising which uses an image of a cartoon character, mascot, action figure, balloon, fruit, or toy;

(E) Each Cannabis retail facility shall regularly offer for sale containers for the storage of Cannabis and Cannabis products which lock and are designed to prohibit children from unlocking and opening the container;

(F) A Cannabis retail facility shall:

(1) Include a written notification with each sale of Cannabis or Cannabis products which advises the purchaser:

(a) To keep Cannabis and Cannabis products out of the reach of children;

(b) That Cannabis products can cause severe illness in children;

(c) That allowing children to ingest Cannabis or Cannabis products or storing Cannabis or Cannabis products in a location which is accessible to children may result in an investigation by an agency which provides child welfare services or criminal prosecution for child abuse or neglect;

(d) That the intoxicating effects of edible Cannabis products may be delayed by two hours or more and users of edible Cannabis products should initially ingest a small amount of the product, then wait at least 120 minutes before ingesting any additional amount of the product;

(e) That pregnant women should consult with a physician before ingesting Cannabis or Cannabis products;

(f) That ingesting Cannabis or Cannabis products with alcohol or other drugs, including prescription medication, may result in unpredictable levels of impairment and that a person should consult with a physician;

(g) That Cannabis or Cannabis products can impair concentration, coordination and judgment and a person should not operate a motor vehicle while under the influence of Cannabis or Cannabis products; and

(h) That ingestion of any amount of Cannabis or Cannabis products before driving may result in criminal prosecution for driving under the influence.

(G) Enclose all Cannabis and Cannabis products in opaque, child-proof packaging upon sale;

(H) A Cannabis retail facility shall not allow any person who is at less than twenty-one (21) years of age to enter the premises of the Cannabis retail facility;

(I) A Cannabis cultivation facility or a Cannabis manufacturing facility may sell a commodity or product made using hemp or containing cannabidiol to a Cannabis retail facility;

(J) In addition to any other product authorized by the provisions of this Act, a Cannabis retail facility may sell:

(1) Any commodity or product made using hemp;

(2) Any commodity or product containing cannabidiol with a THC concentration of not more than 0.3 percent; and

(3) Any other product specified by regulation of the Commission;

(K) A Cannabis facility:

(1) Shall not engage in advertising which contains any statement or illustration that:

(a) Is false or misleading;

(b) Promotes overconsumption of Cannabis or Cannabis products;

(c) Depicts the actual consumption of Cannabis or Cannabis products; or

(d) Depicts a child or other person who is less than twenty-one (21) years of age consuming Cannabis or Cannabis products or objects suggesting the presence of a child, including, without limitation, toys, characters, or cartoons, or contains any other depiction which is designed in any manner to be appealing to or encourage consumption of Cannabis or Cannabis products by a person who is less than twenty-one (21) years of age;

(e) Shall not place a Cannabis advertisement:

(i) Within 1,000 feet of a school, playground, public park, or library, but may maintain such an advertisement if it was initially placed before the school, playground, public park, or library was located within 1,000 feet of the location of the advertisement;

(ii) On or inside of a motor vehicle used for public transportation or any shelter for public transportation;

(iii) At a sports event to which persons who are less than twenty-one (21) years of age are allowed entry; or

(iv) At an entertainment event if it is reasonably estimated that 30 percent or more of the persons who will attend that event are less than twenty-one (21) years of age.

(f) Shall not advertise or offer any Cannabis or Cannabis product as "free" or "donated" without a purchase;

(g) Shall ensure that all advertising by the Cannabis facility contains such warnings as may be prescribed by the Commission, which must include, without limitation, the following words:

(i) "Products should be kept out of reach of children"; and

(ii) "Products may only be used by adults twenty-one (21) years of age and older"; and

(L) In addition to any other penalties provided for by law, the Commission may impose a civil penalty upon a Cannabis facility that violates the provisions of this section as follows:

(1) For the first violation in the immediately preceding two (2) years, a civil penalty not to exceed $1,250.00;

(2) For the second violation in the immediately preceding two (2) years, a civil penalty not to exceed $2,500.00;

(3) For the third violation in the immediately preceding two (2) years, a civil penalty not to exceed $5,000.00; and

(4) For the fourth violation in the immediately preceding two (2) years, a civil penalty not to exceed $10,000.00.

(Res. No. 2026-11.)
