Upper Mattaponi Tribal Code of Law

105 Limitations

(A) Nothing in this title shall operate or be construed to allow the Commonwealth of Virginia or any political subdivision thereof the ability to impose civil, regulatory, or land use laws within the jurisdiction of the Upper Mattaponi Tribe, unless expressly authorized by a Compact.

(B) This title strikes a balance between Tribal self-government and any conflict with federal law by providing a strong and effective regulatory and enforcement system to address threats to public safety, public health, and to law enforcement interest and consistent with federal guidance on these issues.

(C) Nothing in this title shall be construed to permit any person to engage in the following activities or prevent the Tribe or Commission from imposing any civil, criminal, or other penalties for:

(1) Undertaking any task under the influence of Cannabis that would constitute negligence or professional malpractice;

(2) Possessing, selling, or engaging in the use or consumption of Cannabis at any time in violation of or inconsistent with the terms and provisions of this title and any regulations adopted by the Commission including, but not limited to:

(a) Possessing or using Cannabis on a vehicle that provides group transport to a school, Childcare Facility, or Childhood Program, whether or not the vehicle is owned by a governmental entity; and

(b) Possessing or using Cannabis on the grounds of any School, Childcare Facility, at a Childhood Program, or within a School Zone; and

(3) Activities related to cultivation, production, processing, manufacturing, packaging, delivery, transportation, transferring, distribution, sale, possession, consumption, purchase, storage, receipt, and use of Cannabis that are otherwise illegal under Tribal law or any other applicable law.

(D) To the extent practicable, this title shall operate to:

(1) Permit the lawful sale, distribution, possession, use, and consumption of Cannabis to adults twenty-one (21) years of age or older;

(2) Authorize and permit the lawful cultivation, production, processing, manufacturing, packaging, delivery, transportation, transferring, distribution, sale, possession, consumption, purchase, storage, receipt, and use of Cannabis and Cannabis Products by the Commission;

(3) Prevent the distribution of Cannabis to persons under twenty-one (21) years of age;

(4) Prevent revenue from the sale of Cannabis from going to Criminal Enterprises, gangs, and cartels;

(5) Prevent Cannabis activity that is legal under this title from being used as a cover or pretext for the trafficking of other illegal drugs or other illegal activity;

(6) Prevent violence and the use of firearms in the cultivation and distribution of Cannabis;

(7) Prevent driving under the influence of Cannabis and the exacerbation of other adverse public health consequences associated with the use of Cannabis;

(8) Prevent the growing of Cannabis on lands open to the public and the attendant public safety and environmental dangers posed by Cannabis production on lands open to the public.

(E) This title shall not be construed to:

(1) Prohibit a recipient of a federal grant or an applicant for a federal grant from prohibiting the manufacture, delivery, possession, or use of Cannabis to the extent necessary to satisfy federal requirements for the grant, unless such provision would be specifically required by said grant;

(2) Prevent a party to a federal contract or a person applying to be a party to a federal contract from prohibiting the manufacture, processing, packaging, delivery, possession, or use of Cannabis to the extent necessary to comply with the terms and conditions of the contract or to satisfy federal requirements for the contract;

(3) Require a person to violate a tribal, state, or federal law or exempt a person from a tribal, state, or federal law or obstruct the enforcement of a tribal, state, or federal law;

(4) Permit any person from operating any type of motor vehicle under the influence of Cannabis; or

(5) Permit the use or possession of Cannabis in Schools, jails or correctional facilities, Childcare Facilities, or at a Childhood Programs.

(F) No person or entity may purchase, cultivate, manufacture, produce, possess, process, package, store, transport, deliver, transfer, or sell Cannabis or Cannabis products within the Upper Mattaponi Tribal lands, unless licensed to do so by the Commission. The foregoing shall not be interpreted to prevent adults twenty-one (21) years of age or older from purchasing, possessing, transporting, and using Cannabis in accordance with this title or regulations adopted by the Commission.

(G) Tribal law continues to regulate the cultivation, production, processing, manufacturing, packaging, delivery, transportation, transferring, distribution, sale, possession, consumption, purchase, storage, receipt, and use of Cannabis products on the Upper Mattaponi Tribal lands, and all other conflicting uses under state or federal law, unless authorized by this title, the Commission, or any regulations adopted by the Commission, are unlawful.

(H) Any person and/or entity that fails to produce a License issued by the Commission upon demand—evidencing the lawful cultivation, production, processing, manufacturing, packaging, delivery, transportation, transferring, distribution, sale, possession, consumption, purchase, storage, receipt, and use of Cannabis and/or Cannabis Products—is prima facie evidence that such purported Adult-use Cannabis and/or Cannabis Products is illegal.

(Res. No. 2026-11.)
