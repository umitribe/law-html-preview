Upper Mattaponi Tribal Code of Law

208 Commission Regulations

(A) The Commission shall be responsible for drafting and implementing regulations to fulfill the purposes of this title. Such regulations shall include specific provisions relating to:

(1) Financial requirements for licensees;

(2) Establishing such investigative and enforcement mechanisms as the Commission deems necessary to ensure the compliance of a licensee or agent with the provisions of this title;

(3) Requirements for licensees or agents relating to the cultivation, processing, manufacture, transport, distribution, testing, study, advertising, and sale of Cannabis and Cannabis products;

(4) Reasonable restrictions on the signage, marketing, display, and advertising of Cannabis facilities;

(5) Prescribe the form and any additional required content of applications for licenses issued pursuant to this title;

(6) Establish procedures for the suspension or revocation of a license or other disciplinary action to be taken against a licensee or agent; and

(7) Set forth rules pertaining to the safe and healthful operation of Cannabis facilities, including, without limitation:

(a) The manner of protecting against diversion and theft without imposing an undue burden on Cannabis facilities or compromising the confidentiality of consumers;

(b) Minimum requirements for the oversight of Cannabis facilities;

(c) Minimum requirements for keeping records by Cannabis facilities;

(d) Minimum requirements for the conduct of Cannabis agents;

(e) Provisions for the security of Cannabis facilities, including without limitation, requirements for the protection by a fully operational security alarm system of each Cannabis facility;

(f) Procedures pursuant to which Cannabis facilities must use the services of Cannabis laboratories to ensure that any Cannabis or Cannabis product sold by a Cannabis retail facility to an end user is tested for content, quality, and potency in accordance with standards established by the Commission;

(g) Establish circumstances and procedures pursuant to which the maximum fees set forth in this title may be reduced over time to ensure that the fees are, insofar as may be practicable, revenue neutral;

(h) As far as possible while maintaining accountability, protect the identity, and personal identifying information of each person who receives, facilitates, or delivers services in accordance with this title;

(i) The establishment of a schedule of fees for the issuance of licenses and acceptable methods of payment; and

(j) Address such other matters as the Commission deems necessary to carry out the provisions of this title.

(8) The regulations shall not conflict with, or be held to limit or waive, any aspect of this title.

(9) The Commission shall present the regulations, and any later amendment thereto, to the Chief of the Upper Mattaponi Tribe for approval by Executive Order. If the Chief does not approve or disapprove of the proposed regulations within 30 days of receipt, the proposed regulations shall be considered disapproved. If the proposed regulations are disapproved by the Chief, the Tribal Council may override said disapproval within 30 days of disapproval by a two-thirds majority vote of the Tribal Council.

(10) Once approved, the Commission's regulations shall have the full force of the law.

(11) It is the duty of all licensees, and agents, under the Act to keep abreast of any amendments to this title and/or the Commission's regulations.

(Res. No. 2026-11.)
