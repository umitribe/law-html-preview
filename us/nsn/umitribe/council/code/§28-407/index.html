Upper Mattaponi Tribal Code of Law

407 Agents Required to Register with Board, Requirements

(A) Except as otherwise provided in this section, a person shall not own, manage, operate, work at, be employed by, volunteer, or be an independent contractor or sub-contractor for Cannabis-related services to a Cannabis facility unless the person is licensed as a Cannabis agent pursuant to this title.

(B) A person who wishes to receive an agent license shall submit to the Commission an application on a form prescribed by the Commission. The application must be accompanied by:

(1) The full legal name, physical and mailing address, and date of birth of the prospective Cannabis agent;

(2) A statement signed by the prospective Cannabis agent pledging not to dispense or otherwise divert Cannabis to any person who is not authorized to possess Cannabis in accordance with the provisions of this title;

(3) A statement signed by the prospective Cannabis establishment agent asserting that they have not previously had a Cannabis agent license revoked;

(4) Any information required by the Commission to complete an investigation into the background of the prospective Cannabis agent;

(5) The application fee; and

(6) Such other information as the Commission may require by regulation.

(C) The Commission may conduct any investigation of a prospective Cannabis agent and, for an independent contractor, each employee of the prospective Cannabis agent who will provide labor as a Cannabis agent, that the Commission deems appropriate. In connection with such an investigation, the Commission may:

(1) Conduct or accept any background check the Commission determines to be reliable and expedient to determine the criminal history of the prospective Cannabis agent or the employee;

(2) Require a prospective Cannabis agent, if a natural person, and each employee of a prospective Cannabis agent who will provide labor as a Cannabis agent to submit to the Commission a complete set of fingerprints and written permission authorizing the Commission to submit to the Federal Bureau of Investigation for its report; and

(3) If the Commission imposes the requirement described in paragraph (2), submit the fingerprints of the prospective Cannabis agent and each employee of the prospective Cannabis agent who will provide labor as a Cannabis agent to the Federal Bureau of Investigation for its report.

(D) A Cannabis facility shall notify the Commission within five (5) days after a Cannabis agent ceases to hold an ownership interest in the Cannabis facility, be employed by, volunteer at, or provide labor as a Cannabis agent to the Cannabis facility.

(E) A person who has been convicted of an excluded offense, is less than twenty-one (21) years of age, or is not qualified in the determination of the Commission shall not serve as a Cannabis agent.

(F) If an applicant for a Cannabis agent license satisfies the requirements of this section, is found to be qualified by the Commission, and is not disqualified from serving as such an agent pursuant to this section or any other applicable law, the Commission shall issue to the person a Cannabis agent license. A Cannabis agent license expires two (2) years after the date of issuance and may be renewed upon:

(1) Resubmission of the information set forth in this section; and

(2) Payment of the renewal fee.

(G) A Cannabis agent license issued pursuant to this section to an independent contractor or an employee of an independent contractor authorizes the independent contractor or employee to provide labor to any Cannabis facility within the Tribe's jurisdiction.

(H) A Cannabis agent license issued pursuant to this section to a person who wishes to volunteer or work at a Cannabis facility authorizes the person to volunteer or work at any Cannabis facility within the Tribe's jurisdiction for which the category of the Cannabis agent license authorizes the person to volunteer or work.

(I) Except as otherwise prescribed by regulation of the Commission, an applicant for registration or renewal of an agent license is deemed temporarily registered as a Cannabis agent on the date on which a complete application for registration or renewal is submitted to the Commission. A temporary registration as an agent expires sixty (60) days after the date upon which the application is received.

(Res. No. 2026-11.)
