Upper Mattaponi Tribal Code of Law

708 Contempt

(A) If any person in proceedings before the Commission disobeys or resists any lawful order or refuses to respond to a subpoena, or refuses to take the oath or affirmation as a witness or thereafter refuses to be examined, or is guilty of misconduct during the hearing or so near the place thereof as to obstruct the proceeding, the Commission may certify the facts to the Upper Mattaponi Trial Court.

(B) The Trial Court shall thereupon issue an order directing the person to appear before the Trial Court and show cause why the person should not be punished as for contempt.

(C) The Trial Court order and a copy of the statement of the Commission must be served on the person cited to appear. Thereafter, the Trial Court has jurisdiction of the matter, and the same proceedings must be had, the same penalties may be imposed and the person charged may purge himself or herself of the contempt in the same way as in the case of a person who has committed a contempt in the trial of a civil action before the Trial Court.

(Res. No. 2026-11.)
