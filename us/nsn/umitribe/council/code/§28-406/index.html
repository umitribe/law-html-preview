Upper Mattaponi Tribal Code of Law

406 Cannabis Wholesaler Licenses

(A) A person shall not engage in the wholesale of Cannabis products unless the person holds a Cannabis Wholesaler license issued by the Commission pursuant to this section. The Commission is the sole issuer of Cannabis Wholesaler licenses. Sale of wholesale Cannabis products without the applicable Cannabis Wholesaler license issued by the Commission is unlawful.

(B) A person who wishes to engage in the wholesale of Cannabis products to a Cannabis facility must submit to the Commission an application as prescribed by the Commission.

(C) After receiving a complete application to engage in the wholesale of Cannabis products to a Cannabis facility, the Commission may deny or approve the application and issue a Cannabis Wholesaler license and an alphanumeric identification number if:

(1) The person who wishes to engage in the wholesale of Cannabis products to a Cannabis facility has submitted to the Commission all of the following:

(a) The application fee; and

(b) Application, which must include:

(i) The legal name of the proposed licensee;

(ii) The principal place of business of the proposed licensee, including the State, County, and/or Tribal Affiliation;

(iii) Evidence that the Cannabis Wholesaler is licensed to sell Cannabis in the jurisdiction of its principal place of business, along with copies of all licenses to sell Cannabis issued to the proposed licensee; and

(iv) The full legal name, address, and date of birth of each person who is an owner, officer, or board member of the proposed licensee.

(c) Operating procedures consistent with Commission regulations for oversight of the proposed Cannabis facility, including, without limitation:

(i) Procedures to ensure the use of adequate security measures;

(ii) The use of an electronic verification system and an inventory control system pursuant to this title; and

(iii) Procedures for handling of Cannabis products which must be preapproved by the Commission

(d) Such other information or materials as the Commission may require by regulation.

(2) None of the owners, officers, or board members of the proposed licensee have been convicted of an excluded offense;

(3) None of the persons who are owners, officers, or board members of the proposed licensee have:

(a) Served as an owner, officer, or board member for a Cannabis facility that has had its Cannabis facility license, or equivalent, revoked in any jurisdiction; and

(b) Previously had a Cannabis agent license revoked, or equivalent, in any jurisdiction.

(4) None of the persons who are owners, officers, or board members of the proposed licensee are under twenty-one (21) years of age.

(D) Except as otherwise provided in this section, if an application satisfies the requirements of this section, is qualified in the determination of the Commission, and the proposed licensee is not disqualified from being registered as a Cannabis Wholesaler pursuant to this section or other applicable law, the Commission shall issue to the applicant a Cannabis Wholesaler license. A Cannabis Wholesaler license expires one (1) year after the date of issuance and may be renewed upon:

(1) Submission of the information required by the Commission by regulation; and

(2) Payment of the renewal fee.

(E) In determining whether to issue a Cannabis Wholesaler license pursuant to this section, the Commission shall consider the criteria of merit set forth in this title.

(F) A Cannabis Wholesaler license is not a substitute for a Tribal business license. Licensees must abide by all applicable Tribal laws, rules, and regulations at all times.

(G) The Commission, by regulation, may adopt regulations prescribing requirements for the issuance of licenses to business entities and standards for licensees that are business entities which are more stringent than the requirements and standards otherwise set forth in this title.

(Res. No. 2026-11.)
