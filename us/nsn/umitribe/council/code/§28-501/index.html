Upper Mattaponi Tribal Code of Law

501 Operating Documents, Security, Inspections

(A) The operating documents of a Cannabis facility must include procedures:

(1) For the oversight of the Cannabis facility; and

(2) To ensure accurate recordkeeping.

(B) Except as otherwise provided in this section, a Cannabis facility:

(1) That is a Cannabis retail facility must have a single entrance for patrons, which must be secure, and shall implement strict security measures to deter and prevent the theft and unauthorized entrance into areas containing Cannabis; and

(2) That is not a Cannabis retail facility must have secure entrances and shall implement strict security measures to deter and prevent the theft and unauthorized entrance into areas containing Cannabis.

(C) All cultivation or processing of Cannabis that a Cannabis cultivation facility carries out or causes to be carried out must take place in an enclosed, locked area at the physical address provided to the Commission during the licensing process for the Cannabis facility. Such an enclosed, locked area must be accessible only by Cannabis agents who are lawfully associated with the Cannabis cultivation facility, except that limited access by other persons is permissible if such persons are supervised by a Cannabis agent of the Cannabis facility.

(D) Cannabis facilities are subject to reasonable inspection by the Commission at any time, and a person who holds a license must make himself or herself, or a designee thereof, available and present for any inspection by the Commission of the Cannabis establishment.

(E) Each Cannabis facility shall install a video monitoring system which must, at a minimum:

(1) Allow for the transmission and storage, by digital or analog means, of a video feed which displays the interior and exterior of the Cannabis facility; and

(2) Shall store recordings of video feeds for a period of at least ninety (90) days.

(Res. No. 2026-11.)
