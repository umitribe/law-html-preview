Upper Mattaponi Tribal Code of Law

402 Licensing Cannabis Facilities

(A) A person shall not engage in the business of a Cannabis facility unless the person holds a Cannabis facility license issued by the Commission pursuant to this section. The Commission is the sole issuer of Cannabis facility licenses. Operating a Cannabis facility without the applicable Cannabis facility license issued by the Commission is unlawful.

(B) A person who wishes to engage in the business of a Cannabis facility must submit to the Commission an application as prescribed by the Commission.

(C) After receiving a complete application to engage in the business of a Cannabis facility, the Commission may deny or approve the application and register the Cannabis facility and issue a Cannabis facility license and an alphanumeric identification number if:

(1) The person who wishes to operate the proposed Cannabis facility has submitted to the Commission all of the following:

(a) The application fee; and

(b) Application, which must include:

(i) The legal name of the proposed Cannabis facility;

(ii) The legal description of the lands where the proposed Cannabis facility will be located and the legal description of lands of any co-owned additional or otherwise associated Cannabis facilities, the locations of which may not be within 1,000 feet of a school that provides formal education traditionally associated with preschool or kindergarten through grade 12 and that existed on the date on which the application for the proposed Cannabis facility was submitted to the Commission, or within 100 feet of a Community Facility or gaming facility that existed on the date on which the application for the proposed Cannabis facility was submitted to the Commission;

(iii) A certification that the location of the proposed Cannabis facility is upon lands of the Upper Mattaponi Tribe, whether held in fee simple or trust;

(iv) Evidence that the applicant controls adequate liquid assets to cover the initial expenses of opening the proposed Cannabis facility and complying with the provisions of this Act;

(v) Evidence that the applicant owns or has lawful possession of the property on which the proposed Cannabis facility will be located or has the written permission of the property owner to operate the proposed Cannabis facility on that property;

(vi) For the applicant and each person who is proposed to be an owner, officer, or board member of the proposed Cannabis facility, a complete set of the person's fingerprints and written permission of the person authorizing the Commission to use the fingerprints for criminal background check purposes, including submission to the Federal Bureau of Investigation for its report; and

(vii) The full legal name, address, and date of birth of each person who is proposed to be an owner, officer, or board member of the proposed licensee.

(c) Operating procedures consistent with Commission regulations for oversight of the proposed Cannabis facility, including, without limitation:

(i) Procedures to ensure the use of adequate security measures; and

(ii) The use of an electronic verification system and an inventory control system pursuant to this Act.

(d) If the proposed Cannabis facility will sell or deliver Cannabis products, proposed operating procedures for handling such products which must be preapproved by the Commission;

(e) Proof that the proposed location is in compliance with all applicable building requirements; and

(f) Such other information or materials as the Commission may require by regulation.

(2) None of the persons who are proposed to be owners, officers, or board members of the proposed Cannabis facility have been convicted of an excluded offense;

(3) None of the persons who are proposed to be owners, officers, or board members of the proposed Cannabis facility have:

(a) Served as an owner, officer, or board member for a Cannabis facility that has had its Cannabis facility license, or equivalent, revoked in any jurisdiction;

(b) Previously had a Cannabis agent license revoked, or equivalent, in any jurisdiction;

(c) Been listed on the United States System for Award Management disbarment list; and

(4) None of the persons who are proposed to be owners, officers, or board members of the proposed Cannabis facility are under twenty-one (21) years of age.

(D) Except as otherwise provided in paragraph (E) of this section, if an application satisfies the requirements of this section, is qualified in the determination of the Commission, and the facility is not disqualified from being registered as a Cannabis facility pursuant to this section or other applicable law, the Commission shall issue to the establishment a Cannabis facility license. A Cannabis facility license expires one (1) year after the date of issuance and may be renewed upon:

(1) Submission of the information required by the Commission by regulation; and

(2) Payment of the renewal fee.

(E) In determining whether to issue a Cannabis facility license pursuant to this section, the Commission shall consider the criteria of merit set forth in this Act.

(F) For the purposes of measuring distances in this section, the distance must be measured from the front door of the proposed Cannabis facility to the front door of a school, Community Facility, or gaming facility.

(G) A Cannabis facility license is not a substitute for a Tribal business license. Licensees must abide by all applicable Tribal laws, rules, and regulations at all times.

(H) The Commission, by regulation, may adopt regulations prescribing requirements for the issuance of licenses to business entities and standards for licensees that are business entities which are more stringent than the requirements and standards otherwise set forth in this Act.

(Res. No. 2026-11.)
